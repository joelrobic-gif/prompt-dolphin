## Logistics

- **Tool for the interview:** Zoom or Teams, with screen share. The prototype URL is sent at the start of the call (it lives on a Railway preview URL — see Phase 0.5 spike).
- **Duration:** 20 minutes hard stop. Better to leave wanting more than to overstay.
- **Note-taking:** Manual, by Joel during the call. No recording, no transcription, no AI note-taker. The privacy promise of the product applies to the interviews about the product.
- **Compensation:** None for warm contacts. A $50 Amazon gift card for cold contacts or second-degree connections, sent within 24 hours of the interview.
- **Follow-up:** Thank-you email within 24 hours. If the contact wants to see the final product, capture their email separately (not in interview notes) and add them to the launch list.
